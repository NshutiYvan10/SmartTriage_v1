One Patient's Journey, End to End

Before the deep dives, follow a single patient through the whole system. Every numbered stop is expanded in its own chapter. Two entrances exist — the front door and the ambulance — and they merge at triage.

3.2Entrance A — the front door

Identify or register.

The registrar taps the patient's RFID card or searches by national ID / MRN / phone / name+DOB. A returning patient is recognized — even from another hospital via the shared identity (§4.4). A new patient is registered (patient + visit created atomically). An unconscious unknown becomes a NATO-phonetic placeholder — “Unknown Alpha” — identified properly later (§4.6).

The safety floor appears.

For any identified patient, staff can see the cross-hospital safety summary: allergies, chronic conditions and active medications from every SmartTriage hospital, each tagged with its source hospital. No consent needed — this is the always-available safety minimum (§14.2).

Queue for triage.

The visit sits in the triage nurse's pre-triage queue (AWAITING_TRIAGE). Waiting-time monitors are already watching (§5.7).

3.3Entrance B — the ambulance

Run starts.

A paramedic opens an EMS run (DISPATCHED), records vitals and interventions on scene.

Field triage.

The paramedic's app computes triage with the same engines the ED uses — no “ambulance math”. A GCS ≤ 8 forces RED. Downgrading a previously-computed category requires explicit acknowledgment.

Pre-registration.

En route, the run pre-registers a visit at the destination (placeholder patient if unidentified). The ED sees an inbound board; a pre-arrival alert fires — CRITICAL if RED or lights-and-sirens, routed to the charge nurse and RESUS.

Arrival + acuity split.

On confirmed arrival: field RED/ORANGE patients go straight to a treatment zone (formal triage follows at the bedside — a re-triage clock of 5 min for RED, 15 min routine, 2 min if uncategorized ensures it happens); YELLOW/GREEN join the normal triage queue.

Transfer of care.

An ED nurse/doctor formally accepts handover (a paramedic can never accept their own). The system writes an immutable attested handover note; the run ends HANDED_OFF. If nobody accepts within 10 min of arrival, an EMS_HANDOVER_PENDING alert fires.

3.4The middle: triage → treatment

Triage (§5).

The triage nurse completes the adult or pediatric national form. The engine computes a TEWS score and a color (RED/ORANGE/YELLOW/GREEN), routes the patient to a zone, usually auto-places them into a suitable bed, and fires zone-routed doctor alerts for RED/ORANGE/YELLOW.

Automatic screenings piggyback.

The same triage save silently runs: baseline clinical-sign capture, hypoglycemia detection, infection-isolation screening, and (from the form's pregnancy answer) pregnancy status capture. None of these can block the triage itself.

Monitoring begins (§7).

Vitals arrive manually or stream from a bedside monitor every 5 s. The continuous-monitoring engine watches for deterioration and fires alerts; worsening clinical signs can auto-re-triage the patient upward (never downward — de-escalation is only ever a suggestion).

The doctor works the chart (§6, §9–10).

Assessment, diagnoses (ICD-coded), lab/imaging orders, prescriptions — each prescription passing the medication safety gates (allergy, interaction, duplicate, pregnancy) — plus notes, consents and documents. Nurses execute: administering doses on the MAR, collecting specimens, running protocol checklists.

Escalation when needed (§11).

ICU escalation (nurse may raise, doctor decides), inter-zone transfers (received zone must accept), referrals to other facilities, and — at any shift boundary — auto-generated handover reports (06:30 and 18:30, before the 07:00/19:00 shift changes).

Disposition also automatically: stops any bedside-monitor session, and releases the bed into CLEANING (a bed is never reusable without the hygiene step, §12).

Tip

If a “finished” patient still shows on the board, the disposition was probably never recorded — closing the chart is not a disposition. Everything downstream (bed release, monitor stop, reports) hangs off the disposition record.
