People & Permissions

SmartTriage describes a person with three separate ideas: a permanent Role (what kind of account), a professional Designation (seniority title), and a per-shift Shift Function (what station they are working right now). Understanding this trio explains nearly every “why can't I click this?” question.

Note

Example: a Senior Nurse (designation) with role NURSE might be CHARGE_NURSE on this morning's roster and ZONE_NURSE this afternoon. “Who's at triage today?” is a question about the roster, not about the person — triage was deliberately removed as a designation (migration V45) for exactly this reason.

2.2.1Designations (seniority titles)

Displayed next to the name everywhere (triage records, notes, alerts). Almost all are display-only. The one exception: CHARGE_NURSE carries real unit-management authority — managing shift assignments and zone coverage for their hospital.

NurseCharge Nurse (authority) · Senior Nurse · Staff Nurse · Student Nurse
LabHead Lab Technician · Lab Technician

Business rule

The server validates the (role, designation) pair at save time — e.g. a DOCTOR account can never hold the CHARGE_NURSE designation and inherit nurse unit-management authority. The UI's filtered dropdown is convenience; Designation.isValidForRole is the control.

2.4Getting in: invitations, activation, login

Admin creates the account.

A HOSPITAL_ADMIN (or SUPER_ADMIN) creates the user with role + designation. Accounts start inactive. (An admin may also supply a password directly — used for test fixtures.)

Invitation is issued.

The user receives an activation link (email delivery configured per environment). The invitation is time-limited.

User activates.

On /activate they set their password; the account flips to active. Expired invitations must be re-issued by the admin.

Login.

/login issues a JWT the web app sends on every request. An inactive or unknown account, or a wrong password, is rejected with a generic message (no username enumeration).

Warning

A freshly-created account that skips activation stays inactive and cannot log in — the most common “my new user can't log in” cause. Check the account's active flag first.

2.5Zone scoping: why staff see only “their” patients

Doctors and nurses do not browse the whole hospital. Their working set is derived from the active shift roster:

  • A zone nurse / primary doctor sees patients whose currentEdZone is among their shift's covered zones (primary + any additional zones).
  • Today's triage nurse sees the pre-triage queue (patients not yet placed in a zone).
  • Cross-zone authority (charge nurse, supervising doctor, admin oversight, READ_ONLY review) sees the full hospital board.
  • Off-shift clinicians see an empty list. Being employed is not the same as being on duty.
  • PARAMEDIC and LAB_TECHNICIAN get an empty general list by design — they have their own worklists.
Note

Zone scoping is a privacy control, not just a convenience filter: it prevents an off-duty or unrelated staff member from browsing patients they aren't treating. Reading a specific chart still works when a patient is directly under your care (or via oversight authority) — and emergency cross-hospital access has its own explicit, audited path (§14).
